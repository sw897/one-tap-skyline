## 年会与现场互动部署

可提供部署服务与活动前使用准备支持，帮助将游戏用于年会、展会、团队活动或现场互动。完成部署和准备后，参与者通过手机扫码游玩，大屏展示共享城市与排行榜。具体协助范围和安排通过留言沟通确认。

咨询时可说明计划使用时间、预计总人数及同时游玩人数、希望获得的协助，以及是否已有服务器、域名、大屏和现场网络条件。先提供可公开的概况即可，无需公开实际部署地址或凭据。

也可以自行搭建，参阅[部署说明](docs/deployment.md)；在电脑上试用模拟玩法可查看[本地运行说明](CONTRIBUTING.md#local-practice)。

## 反馈与资料边界

问题反馈尽量包含复现步骤、实际与预期表现、设备和浏览器；如知道版本，也请注明。截图和示例先脱敏。

请勿在公开留言中填写密钥、密码、完整扫码链接及当日参与码、真实玩家照片、个人联系方式或未经允许公开的活动资料。敏感部署信息通过后续双方约定的私密方式交接，本项目不在公开表单中收集这些资料。

## English

[Try the public simulation](https://sw897.github.io/one-tap-skyline/) without signing in. It keeps data only in the current page; shared scores and photo uploads require the full deployment.

Use the links above for bug reports, feature requests or deployment inquiries. Check existing Issues first and continue related follow-up in the same thread. A GitHub account is required to post; messages in this public repository are public.

Deployment services and preparation support are available for annual gatherings, exhibitions and live events. The intended setup lets participants play on their phones while a big screen shows the city and leaderboard. Leave a message to discuss the scope and arrangements. A public summary of your target date, expected total/concurrent participation, requested help and available equipment is enough to start.

For a bug, include reproduction steps, expected and actual behavior, device/browser and the version if known. Redact examples and screenshots. Do not post credentials, full QR links or daily entry codes, live deployment addresses, player photos, personal contact details or private event materials. Arrange a private channel later for sensitive deployment information.

For self-hosting, see the [deployment guide](docs/deployment.md). Contributors and coding agents should also read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
